How an engagement runs

Typically six to twelve weeks from kickoff to a model running on your hardware.

  1. 01

    Site & data audit

    We review your camera coverage, lighting conditions, and existing footage to scope what's realistic.

  2. 02

    Data & labeling

    We curate and annotate a training set from your real footage, including the edge cases that break generic models.

  3. 03

    Model training

    Custom architectures tuned for your object classes, benchmarked against your accuracy and latency targets.

  4. 04

    Deploy & monitor

    Shipped to your edge devices with drift monitoring, so the model keeps performing as conditions change.
